INORMATION TECHNILOGY

technology

Tony Stark, also known as Iron Man, is a brilliant, wealthy, and highly innovative inventor in the Marvel universe. He is a genius engineer who uses his intelligence and technology to create advanced Iron Man suits. At first, Tony is confident, arrogant, and focused mainly on his own success, but after experiencing life-threatening situations, he begins to understand responsibility and the consequences of his actions. He eventually becomes a hero who uses his intelligence, technology, and courage to protect people. His journey shows how a person can change from a self-centered genius into someone willing to sacrifice himself for others.
